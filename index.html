ARIEL'S '79 CHARGER

ARIEL RACEWAY · 3.6 KM

How to drive · best lap —
  • Hold the phone like a steering wheel (landscape) and tilt to steer. Tap CENTER to re-zero.
  • Pull away: hold CLUTCH → drag the knob (or tap) into 1 → add a little GAS while slowly lifting the clutch.
  • Shift: lift off gas, CLUTCH in, move the knob, clutch out. Shifting without the clutch grinds unless the revs are matched.
  • Pedals are analog: touch near the top to feather, lower for full.
  • 700 hp through skinny '70s tyres — be gentle with the throttle out of corners or the rear will step out.
  • Stalled? Clutch in (or neutral) and tap START.
  • Drag strip: stop with the front wheels just behind the white line in gear — the tree counts down (amber, amber, amber, GREEN). Leave early and it's a red light.
  • Launch control: in 1st at a standstill, hold BRAKE + GAS (revs lock at 3,500), then let go of the brake.
  • E-BRAKE: hold to lock the rear wheels and swing the tail into a hairpin; let go and feed in the gas.
  • NOS: tap to arm, then floor the GAS — it sprays while you're at full throttle and refills over time.
  • Keyboard: W/S/A/D or arrows · Space = clutch · 1-5, R, N · I = ignition · C = camera · X = NOS · H = hand brake · T = reset · Esc = menu.
